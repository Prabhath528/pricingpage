Frequently asked questions

How do payments work?

50% advance before development starts and the remaining 50% after completion, before final handover. Message us for the current payment methods.

What does monthly maintenance include?

Limited, agreed maintenance work to keep your site running smoothly. The first month after launch is free. New development is not part of maintenance and is quoted separately.

Are domain and hosting included?

No. Domain registration, renewals and paid hosting are separate. We recommend registering the domain in your own name, and you own your domain and business data.

What if I need more pages or features?

Extra pages, redesigns, integrations and new features are quoted separately, so you always know the price before we start.

What happens if a maintenance payment is late?

You get reminders first. If it stays unpaid, maintenance and support may be paused after notice. We never delete your website or take over your domain.

Which package should I pick?

Choose Mini Web for a simple presence, Business Web for a fuller business site, Business Plus if you need a database and admin area, and Custom Web for anything bigger. Not sure? Just message us.
